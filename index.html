I’m an architect interested in the environment, astronomy, history, and spatial philosophy.

I’m deeply inspired by deserts and their systems of navigation and timekeeping, with a particular fascination for how language and folklore preserve environmental knowledge.

My professional path moves between architecture, research, academia, and creative direction.

I am currently an artist-in-residence at Diriyah Art Futures in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and a participant in the Sharjah Architecture Triennial 2026.
